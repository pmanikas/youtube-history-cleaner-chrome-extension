# Privacy Policy: Watch History Cleaner for YouTube

Last updated: 17 October 2026

**This extension does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data.**

- It runs entirely in your own browser.
- It only acts on `https://www.youtube.com/` pages, and only after you start a cleanup from its popup.
- It reads the titles, channel names and date headings shown on your watch history page only to decide which items match your choice, and keeps nothing after the page is closed.
- It removes items by clicking YouTube's own "Remove from watch history" controls on your behalf. It does not call any API or server of its own.
- It has no analytics, no tracking, no ads, and loads no remote code.
- It stores no settings or history.

Removed watch history items cannot be restored by this extension.

This extension is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google LLC. YouTube is a trademark of Google LLC.

Questions: contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.
